By the time you see it, it's already spreading

Powdery and downy mildew often begin developing before visible symptoms appear. By the time the first signs are noticeable, infections may already be spreading across your vineyard, making timely intervention more difficult.

Even low levels of powdery mildew can negatively affect grape yield and fruit quality, especially when early infections are not controlled promptly. Under favourable disease conditions, susceptible cultivars can experience substantial losses, with severity depending on cultivar susceptibility, infection timing, and overall disease pressure.
